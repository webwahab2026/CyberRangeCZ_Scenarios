# Linux.Ngioweb Botnet Investigation

## INFO_LEVEL
The network has experienced unusual traffic patterns and potential security incidents. Your task is to investigate and identify any malicious activity, determine its scope, and recommend containment measures.

## ACCESS_LEVEL
You have SSH access to the bot1 host with the following credentials:
- Username: cyrano
- Password: cyrano

## TRAINING_LEVEL 1
**Task:** Identify and investigate an unauthorized or suspicious service responsible for periodic external communication.

**Question:** What is the name of the suspicious service that communicates with an external host?

**Hint:** Check the systemd services and look for any unusual or recently created services.

## TRAINING_LEVEL 2
**Task:** Determine the external host communicating with the compromised system.

**Question:** What is the IP address of the command-and-control server?

**Hint:** Investigate the network connections and logs of the suspicious service identified in the previous task.

## TRAINING_LEVEL 3
**Task:** Identify the system receiving the abnormal volume of network traffic generated by the compromised host.

**Question:** What is the IP address of the system being targeted by the flood attack?

**Hint:** Analyze the network traffic and logs to determine the destination of the flood attack.
