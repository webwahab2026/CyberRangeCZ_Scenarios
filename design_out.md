### INFO_LEVEL
**Scenario Title:** Mirai Botnet Investigation

**Scenario Description:**
You are an incident responder tasked with investigating a compromised network where devices have been turned into bots for DDoS attacks. The malware in question is Linux/Mirai-Hilix, which targets Realtek & Huawei routers and transforms them into remotely controlled bots. Your goal is to identify and mitigate the botnet activity.

**Learning Objectives:**
- Identify unauthorized or suspicious services responsible for periodic external communication.
- Determine the external host communicating with the compromised system.
- Investigate network connections and identify the command-and-control server.
- Analyze network flows to determine the victim of the flooding activity.
- Collect forensic evidence supporting the identified botnet activity.
