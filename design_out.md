# XOR.DDoS Botnet Investigation

## INFO_LEVEL
The organization has detected unusual network traffic patterns originating from several internal systems. Initial analysis suggests these systems may be participating in a botnet. Your task is to investigate and contain this potential threat.

## ACCESS_LEVEL
You have SSH access to the following systems with the username `cyrano` and password `cyrano`:
- `bot1` (192.168.70.10)
- `bot2` (192.168.70.11)

## TRAINING_LEVEL
### Task 1: Suspicious Service Discovery
**Objective:** Identify and investigate an unauthorized or suspicious service responsible for periodic external communication.

**Question:** What is the name of the suspicious service that is making periodic external connections?

**Hint:** Check running services and look for anything unusual or not part of the standard system configuration.

### Task 2: Persistence Discovery
**Objective:** Determine how the suspicious service is automatically started after reboot.

**Question:** What is the name of the systemd service responsible for the persistence of the suspicious service?

**Hint:** Investigate systemd timers and services that might be related to the suspicious activity.

### Task 3: Flood Attack Target Identification
**Objective:** Identify the system receiving the abnormal volume of network traffic generated by the compromised host.

**Question:** What is the IP address of the system being targeted by the flood attack?

**Hint:** Examine network connections and logs to determine the destination of the suspicious traffic.
