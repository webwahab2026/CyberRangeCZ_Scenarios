# Scenario: Linux/Remaiten Botnet Investigation

## INFO_LEVEL
The network has experienced unusual traffic patterns and potential security incidents. Your task is to investigate and respond to these issues.

## ACCESS_LEVEL
You have SSH access to the victim host with the following credentials:
- Hostname: victim
- Username: ubuntu
- Password: ubuntu

## TRAINING_LEVEL 1
**Task:** Identify and investigate an unauthorized or suspicious service responsible for periodic external communication.

**Question:** What is the name of the suspicious service that is beaconing to an external host?

**Hint:** Check running services and look for unusual network connections.

## TRAINING_LEVEL 2
**Task:** Determine the external host communicating with the compromised system.

**Question:** What is the IP address of the command-and-control server?

**Hint:** Investigate network connections and logs for external communication.

## TRAINING_LEVEL 3
**Task:** Identify the system receiving the abnormal volume of network traffic generated by the compromised host.

**Question:** What is the IP address of the system being targeted by the flood attack?

**Hint:** Analyze network traffic to identify the victim of the flooding activity.

---

This scenario is designed to align with the provided MISP event, security profile, topology, and Ansible configuration. The tasks focus on investigating and responding to a Linux/Remaiten botnet infection, with a specific emphasis on identifying suspicious services, command-and-control communication, and flood attack targets. The scenario adheres to the sandbox constraints and uses only the indicators and infrastructure explicitly present in the provided data.
