### Scenario: XOR.DDoS Botnet Investigation

#### INFO_LEVEL
The network has been experiencing unusual traffic patterns and potential command-and-control (C2) communications. Your task is to investigate and mitigate the threat posed by the XOR.DDoS botnet malware.

#### ACCESS_LEVEL
- **Entry Host**: bot1
- **Username**: cyrano
- **Password**: cyrano
- **Access Method**: SSH

#### TRAINING_LEVEL 1
**Task**: Identify and investigate an unauthorized or suspicious service responsible for periodic external communication.
**Objective**: Enumerate running services and identify beaconing behavior associated with botnet activity.
**Hint**: Check the systemd services and look for any unusual or recently added services.
**Validation**: The service name must be identified and reported.

#### TRAINING_LEVEL 2
**Task**: Determine the external host communicating with the compromised system.
**Objective**: Investigate network connections and identify the command-and-control server.
**Hint**: Examine the network connections and logs for any suspicious outbound connections.
**Validation**: The C2 IP address must be identified and reported.

#### TRAINING_LEVEL 3
**Task**: Identify the system receiving the abnormal volume of network traffic generated by the compromised host.
**Objective**: Analyze network flows to determine the victim of the flooding activity.
**Hint**: Review the network traffic logs and look for any unusual destination IPs.
**Validation**: The victim IP address must be identified and reported.
